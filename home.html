Welcome to the King County Transit Accessibility Project

Public transportation plays a crucial role in connecting communities, providing access to jobs, education, and essential services. However, transit access is not evenly distributed across King County.

This interactive Web GIS application allows users to explore transit accessibility throughout King County, identify underserved areas, and support efforts to improve transportation equity.

What You Can Do

  • 🔍 Explore an interactive **transit accessibility map**
  • 📊 View **bus routes, light rail stops, and ferry terminals**
  • 🗺️ Use **heatmaps and data overlays** to see coverage gaps
  • 📈 Analyze **demographic data** related to transit access

Project Goals

  • Map public transit stops and analyze accessibility gaps
  • Show disparities in transit service coverage with interactive layers
  • Provide a user-friendly interface for data-driven transit planning
  • Support advocacy for equitable public transportation
